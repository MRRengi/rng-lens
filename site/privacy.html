rng-lens

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

rng-lens is a free, open source app that turns an Android phone into a webcam and microphone for a computer. It is made by Maksym Zhuk (Rengi Dev). This policy covers the rng-lens phone app and the rng-lens desktop app.

Short version

rng-lens collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera and microphone

The phone app uses the camera and, if you turn it on, the microphone, only to stream the picture and sound to the computer you connected it to. The stream goes directly from your phone to your computer over your own Wi-Fi network or a USB cable. It does not pass through any server. Nothing is recorded or saved by rng-lens on the phone or on the computer.

Network

To find each other, the phone and the computer send small announcement messages on your local network. These messages contain the phone's model name and the port number, and they never leave your local network.

Device information

The phone sends its battery level and temperature to your computer so the desktop app can show them and protect the phone from overheating. This information stays between your own devices.

Permissions

  • Camera and Microphone: to stream to your computer.
  • Internet, network and Wi-Fi state, multicast: to connect to your computer on the local network.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep streaming while the screen is off, with a notification that lets you stop it at any time.

Children

rng-lens does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
